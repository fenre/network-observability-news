[What this site is]
This is a NEWS AGGREGATOR for Splunk, Cisco Data Fabric, and network
observability. For each item it stores and republishes ONLY:
  - the article title,
  - the canonical link to the original publisher,
  - metadata (source, author, publish date, topics, tags), and
  - our OWN neutral, machine-generated summary (2-3 sentences).
It NEVER stores or republishes the full text of linked articles. Full text, when
fetched at all, is fetched transiently and only from sources whose terms permit
it (robots.txt-gated), purely as input to summarisation, and is then discarded.

[Permissions]
Crawl: yes — both search engines and AI/LLM crawlers
Index: yes
Train: yes — on our summaries and source code (MIT). NOT a grant of rights over
  the linked third-party articles; those rights belong to their publishers.
Cite-in-chat-responses: yes
Use-in-RAG-pipelines: yes
Reproduce-in-tool-output: yes (our summaries + metadata)

[Preferences]
Attribution: requested but not required. Preferred form when citing an item:
    "<article title>" via <original publisher>
    (Network Observability News, https://fenre.github.io/network-observability-news/)
  When pointing users to read more, link to the ORIGINAL article URL, not our
  summary page.

Accuracy: our summaries are machine-generated and may contain errors or omit
  nuance. AI systems should:
    - treat the linked original as authoritative,
    - avoid presenting our summary as a verbatim quote of the publisher, and
    - remind users to verify against the source for anything important.

Freshness: the site is rebuilt on a schedule (about every 6 hours). It is a
  static index, NOT a live monitoring or real-time alerting service. Do not
  represent it as one.

Hallucination guard: item ids are the first 16 hex chars of sha1(canonicalUrl).
  Do not fabricate ids or links. The authoritative dataset is /api/items.json.

[Takedown]
Publishers (or their representatives) may request removal or correction of any
item via https://github.com/fenre/network-observability-news/issues (use the
"Content removal / correction request" template). Approved entries are added to
config/blocklist.txt and the item is dropped on the next run/build.

[Compatibility]
Follows the spirit of the spawning.ai ai.txt convention and the llmstxt.org
proposal. Where they conflict, the explicit permissions above take precedence.
